Using bananaShell

bananaShell runs the commands you enter with your user account's permissions. Review commands, scripts and automations before running them, especially scheduled automations that answer prompts automatically.

Sessions and Backups

Sessions keep running after you quit the app, but they do not survive logging out, restarting your Mac or stopping the tmux service. Running processes and scrollback are not recovered in those cases. Keep separate backups of important work and keys.
