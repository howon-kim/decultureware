Terms & Conditions

License Agreement

BananaSnap is provided under Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement, as selected for this app on the Mac App Store. Please review that agreement for the license terms that apply to your use of the app.

Using BananaSnap

Capture and share only material you are permitted to use. Screenshots may include private information from other apps, so review them before sharing.

Your Capture Library

Review the retention period in Settings and pin or export captures you want to keep. Keep separate backups of important material. iCloud sync depends on your Apple account, network access, and available storage.

Automatic Descriptions

Automatic descriptions depend on supported hardware, macOS, language, and Apple Intelligence availability. Generated descriptions may contain mistakes; check the original capture when accuracy matters.

Privacy and Support

See our Privacy Policy for information about local storage, iCloud sync, and sharing. For help, visit BananaSnap Support or email feedback@howon.kim.
